Privacy Policy for PreNote

Last updated: 2026-01-01

Data Collection

PreNote does not collect, store, or transmit personal user data. All notes are stored locally on your device.

Encryption

Notes may be encrypted locally on the device using industry-standard encryption. No encrypted content is transmitted.

Ads

PreNote displays ads provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect limited device information (e.g., advertising ID) according to their own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. PreNote does not store payment information.

Internet Access

Internet access is used only for ad delivery and purchase verification.

Children’s Privacy

PreNote is not designed for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
